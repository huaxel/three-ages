# La Balance identity review

**Decision:** Confirmed as the same building/facade across the four cited sources. The differing address labels are retained as source-specific descriptions; they are not normalized into one asserted street address.

**Reviewer:** User-confirmed in project chat
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-09

## Evidence and limits

- The City of Brussels pilot labels the case “The Weighing Scales,” Grand-Place 24.
- The Brussels architectural heritage inventory identifies La Balance at Rue de la Colline 24 (Urban 30991) and describes its facade as part of the Grand-Place ensemble: <https://monument.heritage.brussels/fr/Bruxelles_Pentagone/Rue_de_la_Colline/24/30991>.
- The British Library engraving caption identifies “Maison de la Balance, rue de la Colline”: <https://www.flickr.com/photos/britishlibrary/11271682895/>.
- The Wikimedia Commons record describes Maison de la Balance at Grand-Place and gives protected-monument identifier 2043-0177/0: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Belgique_-_Bruxelles_-_Maison_de_la_Balance_-_01.jpg>.
- The project source notes report matching distinctive chronology across the City and heritage records (1704, 1890, 1986).

The sources support cross-source identity, not the claim that Rue de la Colline 24 is literally a Grand-Place numbered frontage. Commons' monument identifier is corroborative; it is not treated as the source of the heritage street address. The 1878 British Library item is an engraving, not a photograph, and its date is the publication/item date, not a building-construction date.
