# Three Ages — stakeholder demo script

## Goal

Use the prototype to test whether an evidence-led building-history explorer is useful before requesting image access or creating more labels.

## Five-minute flow

1. **State the question**
   > Can a Brussels building record show separate register, facade and structural evidence without collapsing uncertainty into one date?

2. **Set the boundary**
   Explain that the pilot contains six real Grand Place source records. The source text is real; register dates are explicitly labelled proxies (three Wikidata/heritage-linked and three official architectural-inventory reconstruction dates). Five cases now include licensed 1941–1942 KIK-IRPA facade previews, but image-derived structural annotations are intentionally not final until reviewed.

3. **Select a case**
   Open a case such as Joseph and Anne or The Angel. Point out why it was selected: the history text describes an earlier reconstruction and a later facade rebuild.

4. **Read the three evidence cards**
   - Register age: three cases (The Swan, Joseph and Anne, The Angel) show sourced proxy years traced from Wikidata inception claims to Brussels heritage register records, while The Horn, The Weighing Scales and The Pigeon show direct Brussels architectural-inventory reconstruction-date proxies. The cards link to each evidence source; all remain unreviewed proxies.
   - Historical image previews: five cases show KIK-IRPA source previews with epoch, CC BY 4.0 credit and a link to the catalogue record; the previews are not annotations.
   - Facade evidence: source-described style, not a dated visual classification.
   - Structural evidence: documented reconstruction proxy, not an aerial-image conclusion.

5. **Inspect the evidence trail**
   Show the source history, facade description, next annotation step, dataset endpoint, pilot worksheet export, the two committed ortho previews (1996 CC0 and 2022 open data), and the five KIK-IRPA historical previews with per-asset licence and credit. Emphasise that source previews are not yet reviewed annotations.

6. **Close with the feasibility decision**
   Ask whether the stakeholder can provide a permitted historical view for Grand-Place 24 and whether aerial or equivalent structural evidence is required — the two modern epochs and five KIK-IRPA facade previews are already reusable — then confirm whether the official inventory reconstruction dates meet the intended register-year semantics.

## Questions to capture

- What heritage or planning decision would this explorer support?
- Which building changes matter: facade replacement, rear-volume change, interior reconstruction or complete rebuilding?
- Which image sources are sufficient for reuse? (two modern epochs and five 1941–1942 KIK-IRPA facade previews are secured; Grand-Place 24 and structural/aerial evidence remain open)
- What evidence is sufficient to call a structural claim reviewed?
- Who can perform a second review of annotations?
- Is a six-to-ten-building curated MVP valuable without city-wide automation?

## Success signal

The demo succeeds if the stakeholder accepts the separation between source text, visual annotation and structural evidence, and can identify an approved image source or reviewer. If they require automatic city-wide classification before accepting a curated pilot, revisit the scope before implementation.
