## What to record for each image

- `facade_style` — one controlled value: `baroque`, `classical french`, `renaissance-inspired`, `mixed`, `other`, `uncertain`, `not assessable`.
  Choose what you perceive in the image. The `source_style_text` column is source text, not a visual label.
- `facade_observation` — visible, source-grounded features. Do not infer a construction date from style.
- `reviewer`, `reviewed_at`, `confidence` (`low` / `medium` / `high`).
