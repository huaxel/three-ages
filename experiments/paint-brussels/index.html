WHAT THIS IS — AND ISN’T

An agent-authored architectural study, painted with p5.brush. The building geometry was written from visual inspection of the photograph. No trained painter, automatic facade extraction, epoch prediction, or fidelity evaluation is being claimed.

Palette changes and progressive passes are experiments, not evidence that a style generalizes. The source photograph remains the reference, not the painting.
