A few shortcuts

1 / 2 choose A / B. T both, N neither. Then S similar or U unsure. Hold space for the original. Arrow keys browse the paintings; they never record a choice.

On your phone, swipe between A and B, then tap to choose. Your second tap judges the building’s character separately. Pass means abstain, not dislike.

Choices go automatically to this private server, with a browser backup for interruptions. Choices are not automatically forwarded to a model, and no training runs automatically. This is a personal, private-network tool—not a public multi-user service.
