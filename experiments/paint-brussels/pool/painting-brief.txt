Paint the supplied Brussels photograph by writing a standalone JavaScript program.
Output ONLY JSON: {"title":"...","intent":"...","simplifications":["..."],"program":"function paint(p) { ... }"}.
The program is the artwork. Do NOT output scene JSON, facade-schema parameters, SVG files, HTML, or a description instead of code.

Image 1 is the original photograph. Preserve its recognizable architectural identity and approximate viewpoint. Choose meaningful detail: curved bays, window frames and divisions, cornice profiles, changes in material, parapets, recesses, arches, and spatial depth where visible. Do not invent historical ornament. Richness is not random brick noise. Simplify vegetation and distractions as an artistic decision, not as blank masks covering the whole subject.
The user's taste: strong architectural linework with imperfect printed/painted material; images that BATHE IN COLOUR, not pale outlines on mostly blank paper. Architectural richness matters. Watercolor is NOT required. Interpret; do not make a generic architectural icon.

The 1000x1000 canvas is prepared for you. Use coordinates 0..1000 with origin top-left. No reference pixels are available at runtime. You must paint from your interpretation of the photograph, not trace/filter an embedded image.

The only drawing instrument is p (no building/window/facade helpers):
  p.background(color)
  p.poly([[x,y],...], fill, stroke=null, width=1, alpha=1)
  p.path("SVG path commands incl. M L C Q Z", fill, stroke=null, width=1, alpha=1)
  p.line([[x,y],...], color, width=1, alpha=1)
  p.ellipse(cx,cy,rx,ry,fill,stroke=null,width=1,alpha=1)
  p.brush([[x,y],...],color,width=12,alpha=.7) -- dry, separated bristle marks
  p.hatch(polygonPoints,color,spacing=6,angleDegrees=-45,width=.6,alpha=.3)
  p.grain(color,alpha=.08,count=5000) -- <=20000 grains
  p.rand(min=0,max=1) -- seeded randomness
  p.push(); p.clip(polygonPoints OR SVGpathString); p.pop()
Colors are CSS color strings, fill/stroke may be null. API signatures are exact. No other p methods exist.
Write your own geometric helpers, loops, curves and compositions in JavaScript; those decisions are yours. Use p.path for real curves rather than flattening all rounded architecture. Handle overlapping elements through painting order and p.clip. Keep the whole painting within the canvas.

paint(p) must be synchronous. Use p.rand, never Math.random, Date or timers. No imports, DOM, network, images, eval, Function constructor, or other external resources. No rendered text, captions, score labels or signatures in the image. The renderer has no credentials or source images and enforces a timeout and drawing budget. Stay under 12,000 drawing calls and 64 KiB of code. Prefer expressive, readable code over sheer length.

Do not wrap JSON in markdown. Keep title <=120 characters, intent <=500 characters, and at most eight simplification notes. Do not claim your painting has been independently judged or is historically accurate.
