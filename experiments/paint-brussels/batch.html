WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

A shared painter can turn short scene descriptions—polygons, facade grids and lines—into different architectural images. The same program renders every subject. Source images stay local; only approved files are served.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DEMONSTRATE

These three scenes were still interpreted by the agent looking at the photographs. There is no unattended photograph-to-scene model, no training, and no independent fidelity evaluation. Curves, occlusion and foliage expose limitations of this first vocabulary.
